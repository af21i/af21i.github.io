About Me

Hi, I’m Afeef, a Computer Engineering student at Toronto Metropolitan University. I’m interested in the space where software, hardware, data, and user experience overlap. My projects range from database systems and Java applications to FPGA-based CPU design, embedded robotics, and machine learning dashboards.

I enjoy building things that are more than just assignments. I like when a project has a clear purpose, a polished interface, and enough technical depth to teach me something new. Recently, I’ve been especially interested in real-time analytics, predictive models, and tools that make complicated systems easier to understand visually.

Outside of coding and coursework, I’m heavily involved in student life and event planning. I’ve helped organize engineering events, mentor students, and lead initiatives that bring people together. When I’m not working on something technical, there’s a good chance I’m watching NBA games or spending time with my birds.
